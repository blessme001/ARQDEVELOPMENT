Who We Are

About Aurora
Energy Services

Aurora Energy specializes in delivering OIMR and asset integrity maintenance services across the Gulf of Guinea Basin. Our experienced team ensures the highest standards of quality and safety in all operations — from offshore project planning and execution to subsea inspections and port management.

Headquartered in Houston, Texas — the world's energy capital — we combine world-class technical expertise with deep regional knowledge to drive operational excellence for Africa's offshore energy sector.

What We Do

Our Services

Specializing in energy and industrial project development for emerging markets across Africa's offshore basin.

01

Engineering & Project Management

Comprehensive onshore and offshore engineering services covering every phase of the project lifecycle.

  • Project planning, execution & installation
  • Completions, commissioning & startup
  • Plug and abandonment / decommissioning
  • Skilled manpower supply & management
02

Marine & Subsea Solutions

Advanced ROV services and diving expertise for precise underwater inspections and interventions in challenging environments.

  • Advanced ROV inspections & interventions
  • Air and saturation diving operations
  • Subsea infrastructure maintenance
  • Pipeline and platform inspection
03

Asset Integrity & Risk Management

Specialized subsea construction and maintenance delivered by highly skilled, proficiency-certified personnel.

  • Structure repair, monitoring & CP/CD surveys
  • FPSO hull & turret inspections
  • Leak investigation & remediation
  • Flooded member detection surveys
  • Anchor chain inspection & measurement
04

Port Management Services

Ensuring safe and efficient maritime operations with full compliance with all safety regulations and best practices.

  • Vessel Traffic Services (VTS)
  • Piloting with Tugs and AHTS vessels
  • Buoy & navigational system installation
  • Bathymetric surveys & dredging
  • Marine safety solutions
