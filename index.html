Who We Are

About Aurora Energy Services

Aurora Energy specializes in delivering OIMR and asset integrity maintenance services across the Gulf of Guinea Basin. Our experienced team is dedicated to ensuring the highest standards of quality and safety in all our operations — from offshore project planning and execution to subsea inspections and port management.

Headquartered in Houston, Texas, we combine world-class technical expertise with deep regional knowledge, providing end-to-end marine services that drive operational excellence for Africa's energy sector.
